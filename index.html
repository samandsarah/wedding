Getting there

Stay close to the sand

⌂

Where to stay

Número Uno is our home base. We have reserved its rooms and apartments for Friday and Saturday; please reach out to us about space. Otherwise, we recommend a hotel or Airbnb within walking distance in Ocean Park.

✈

Flying in

Fly into Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport (SJU). Número Uno is roughly a 10-minute drive away; taxi and rideshare are the easiest options.

→

For the weekend

Arrive Friday—or earlier and make a vacation of it. The main Saturday venues are walkable, so you will not need a car for the celebration itself.

Good to know

A few questions, answered

Why Puerto Rico?+

Ten years ago, our first trip together took us to Culebra. We have returned many times since and could not think of a better place to celebrate our marriage.

Will you already be married?+

Yep! We married in a small family ceremony in Cambridge in July. Puerto Rico is the big, relaxed celebration with our wider circle of family and friends.

What should I bring on Saturday?+

Swimwear, sunscreen, sandals, and whatever you like for a beach day. Leave time to change before sunset cocktails and dinner.

Are the times final?+

Friday evening and Saturday dinner are planned. Beach-day, toast, and Sunday times are still being finalized; check this page again before traveling.

Who can help with RSVP or lodging questions?+
